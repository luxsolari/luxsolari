## Hi there 👋 I'm Lux Solari.
_(Also known in _realspace_ as **Luciano Laje**)._

Software engineer from Buenos Aires, Argentina, working as an AI Engineer — building software with AI assistance, learning with it, and shipping on that footing. Working professionally since 2016, through consulting firms including Accenture and Globant.

🔭 Currently at **Mercado Libre (MELI)**, researching and developing GenAI initiatives and tooling enablement for development acceleration.

### 🧰 What I work in

- **Python** — most of what I write now, at work and outside it. Still shedding Java habits.
- **C/C++** — tooling, scripting, CLI work, and the lower-level side of AI engineering.
- **Swift/SwiftUI** — learning it, aiming to ship my own software for Apple platforms.

I work with AI assistance across all of it. In these three I keep it on a short leash: I want to understand every line that ships. Outside them, when a project genuinely needs something else, I lean on it harder. Java is where I have the most mileage, and it still comes out when it's the right tool for the job.
